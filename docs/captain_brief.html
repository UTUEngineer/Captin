Status: Production-Ready Foundations + AI Active

1. What is Captain & What Problem Does It Solve?

Traditional football analysis apps force coaches to choose between two extremes:

1. Flat 2D whiteboards: Static, unable to show actual player perspectives, blind spots, or passing angles.
2. Expensive video software: Clunky, disconnected from whiteboard tactics, requiring hours of manual video tagging.

Captain bridges this divide by providing an all-in-one ecosystem: an interactive 3D tactical pitch with a First-Person "Be The Player" camera, frame-by-frame synchronized match video analysis, live league lineup imports, and automated AI tactical scouting powered by Claude 3.5 Sonnet.

2. What Has Been Built (Detailed Features Completed)

Hub 1: 3D Tactical Playground & First-Person POV Implemented
Built with Flutter 3D and Three.js engines (VirtualPlaygroundScreen.dart)
  • Interactive 3D Pitch: Dynamic orbit, pan, pitch tilt, and zoom camera controls around a photorealistic football turf.
  • "Be The Player" First-Person POV: With one click, the camera drops directly into the chosen player's eye level (1.75 meters) to show coaches what the player actually sees in real match conditions.
  • Formations Engine: Animated transitions between 4-3-3, 4-2-3-1, 3-5-2, 4-4-2, and 5-3-2.
  • Tactical Keyframe Sequence Engine: Coaches record multi-phase plays (e.g. Build-up ➔ Overlap ➔ Cross); the engine automatically interpolates player paths and plays them back smoothly.
  • Supabase Cloud Boards: Save tactics to the cloud database with full formation parameters.
Hub 2: AI Video vs. 3D Simulation Sync Implemented
Frame-accurate sync between match video and 3D space (VideoMatchAnalysisScreen.dart)
  • Dual-Screen Synchronized Playback: Real match footage is synchronized with 3D player nodes at 25 FPS.
  • 22-Player Tracking Ingestion: Connects to computer vision models (YOLOv8 + ByteTrack) to trace all 22 player trajectories plus the ball.
  • 3D Dynamic Heatmaps: Visual density clouds rendered directly over the 3D grass showing positional domination.
  • Passing Risk Corridors: Dynamic color vectors between players showing safe vs. contested passing lanes.
Hub 3: League Scouting & Claude 3.5 AI Advisor Implemented
Live data and AI tactical dossiers (LeagueScoutingScreen.dart)
  • Global & Local Leagues: Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Champions League, and the Iraq Stars League (Al-Zawraa, Al-Shorta, Al-Quwa Al-Jawiya) with real player rosters.
  • 1-Click Squad Ingestion: Ingest official matchday starting XIs directly onto the 3D tactical pitch.
  • Claude 3.5 Sonnet Scouting Engine: Generates instant UEFA Pro analysis identifying opponent defensive vulnerabilities, primary offensive threats, recommended counter-tactics, and key players to press.
  • Automated PDF Reports: Generates high-res tactical reports with Arabic (Cairo font) & English typography.

3. What Needs To Be Done Next (Actionable Roadmap)

Here is the prioritized engineering roadmap organized by delivery urgency:

Phase & Priority Feature / Initiative What It Delivers & Why It Matters
Phase 1 • Critical Automated Pitch Homography Replaces manual 4-point pitch corner clicks with automated keypoint pitch detection (penalty boxes, center circle) via computer vision so coaches don't have to calibrate videos manually.
Phase 1 • High Real-Time Multi-Coach Collaboration Connects Supabase Realtime WebSockets so the head coach and assistant analysts on different iPads/laptops can see player movements and annotations live during halftime talks.
Phase 1 • High Offline-First Storage Fallback Provides full local SQLite/Hive caching for saved tactics, formations, and scouting reports so coaches can work on the pitch without Wi-Fi or cellular signal.
Phase 2 • Medium xG & xT Quantitative Analytics Overlays Expected Goals (xG) shot maps and Expected Threat (xT) passing vectors, plus automatic calculation of defensive compactness area (m²).
Phase 2 • Medium Live Matchday Bench Mode A high-contrast tablet interface for the dugout during matches: 1-touch event logger (fouls, pressing errors) and a quick drag-and-drop substitution impact preview.
Phase 2 • Planned Voiceover Tactical Notes Allows coaches to record short voice memos attached directly to animated keyframe steps for players to listen to before games.
Phase 3 • Long-Term Cloud GPU Cluster & App Store Launch Containerized FastAPI + TensorRT backend on AWS/RunPod for rapid 90-minute video processing, followed by iOS App Store and Google Play Store rollout.
